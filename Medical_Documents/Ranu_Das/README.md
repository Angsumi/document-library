# Ranu Das — medical records, 2002–2014

- **[Ranu_Das_Medical_Records_Chronological.pdf](Ranu_Das_Medical_Records_Chronological.pdf)** — 199 records in date order (2002–2014; a few undated papers last), each with a copyable text transcription followed by the source photo, plus an index.
- **[INDEX.csv](INDEX.csv)** — one row per photo: date, whether the date is uncertain, document, provider, handwritten yes/no, uncertainty note, original filename, renamed file, SHA-256.
- **Originals/** — 199 original photos, byte-for-byte unchanged, renamed `YYYY-MM-DD_NNN_Document.jpg` (NNN = chronological position). Originals are not rotated; the PDF shows them upright.
- **Not_Ranu_Das/** — 2 photos that belong to other people (a pharmacy memo in another name, and Bubul Das's USG-neck envelope).

Typed reports were transcribed directly. Handwritten prescriptions, receipts and notes are best-effort; items marked [?] are uncertain, and receipts' dates/amounts are often uncertain — check against the photo. Content: breast-cancer surgery (Sept 2002), pathology/receptor reports, chemotherapy and radiotherapy 2002–03, tamoxifen/letrozole follow-up with serial ultrasounds to 2009, gall-bladder surgery Dec 2014, plus pharmacy/hospital receipts and 2014 health-insurance papers. Phone numbers are omitted from the transcriptions but visible in the photos.
