                                                                                         German farms between 2016 and 2022 to identify environmental and host
                                                                                         determinants of ARG occurrence. Using joint species distribution models, we

                                                                                         8%. Analysis of ARG traits revealed that agricultural land use and exposure to
                                                                                         livestock increased the occurrence of potentially mobile ARGs. Pig density was

                                                                                         tinctive resistome, but share more than 50% of ARGs with livestock manure,

                                                                                         that landscape conditions, particularly farming intensity, shape the distribu-
                                                                                         tion of speciﬁc ARGs and potentially mobile ARGs in house mice microbiomes.
