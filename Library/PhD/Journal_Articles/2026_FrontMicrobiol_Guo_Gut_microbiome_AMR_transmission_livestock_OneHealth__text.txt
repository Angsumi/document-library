                                             Introduction
                                                  The escalating global crisis of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) represents a formidable
                                             threat to the efficacy of infectious disease treatments in both human and veterinary medicine.
                                             This challenge is intrinsically linked to the complex microbial ecosystems residing within the
                                             gastrointestinal tracts of animals. In particular, companion animals and livestock serve as criti-
                                             cal reservoirs and amplifiers of antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs). The gut microbiome func-
                                             tions not only as a natural repository for diverse ARGs but also as a hotspot for horizontal gene
