Learning Objectives: In this module, we will build a Solid Foundation of Statistics for Data Science, Learn
Probability, Distributions, Hypothesis Testing. Statistics is the science of assigning a probability to an event based on
experiments. It is the application of quantitative principles to the collection, analysis, and presentation of numerical
data. Ace the fundamentals of Data Science, statistics, and Machine Learning with this course. It will enable you to
define statistics and essential terms related to it, explain measures of central tendency and dispersion, and comprehend
skewness, correlation, regression, distribution. You will be able to make data-driven predictions through statistical
inference.
