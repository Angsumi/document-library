Subject: Consent and undertaking regarding non-availing of UGC-NET JRF Fellowship

Respected Sir/Madam,

I, Mr. Angsuman Das, UGC-NET JRF Scholar, hereby submit my consent and undertaking
in connection with the transfer of my UGC-NET Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) from
Madhabdev University to Mizoram University/Pachhunga University College, Aizawl.

I have taken admission to the Ph.D. Programme in Life Sciences at Pachhunga University
College, a constituent college of Mizoram University, and have initiated the necessary
formalities  for transfer and continuation of my UGC-NET JRF Fellowship at the new
institution.

In accordance with the communication received from the concerned UGC authority, I hereby
confirm and undertake that I shall not avail, claim, or draw the UGC-NET JRF Fellowship
from Madhabdev University for any period after my discontinuation/relieving from the
University and transfer of my fellowship to Mizoram University/Pachhunga University
College.

I further declare that  I shall not claim fellowship for the same period from both
institutions, and there shall be no duplication or overlapping claim of fellowship arising out
of the transfer.

Therefore,  if any fellowship payment confirmation or related processing  is pending at
Madhabdev University pertaining to the period after my discontinuation/transfer,  I hereby
give my consent for the same to be treated accordingly for completing the fellowship transfer
formalities.

I kindly request the University to take this undertaking on record and initiate/complete the
necessary  formalities  for  transfer  of my UGC-NET  JRF  Fellowship  to  Mizoram
University/Pachhunga University College.

I shall be grateful for your kind cooperation and necessary action.
