Important: Home Office (UKVI) restrictions

We reserve the right to make amendments to any offers in order to meet the requirements set by UK
Government policies. The Home Office restricts the total time that students may spend in the UK to a
maximum of five years for the majority of undergraduate courses.

If you have applied to study a campus-based course you must complete our UKVI Student Route Eligibility
Assessment, which allows us to determine your eligibility to study in the UK under Home Office immigration
regulations. The assessment must be completed before your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
statement is issued, which you will need to support your Student Route Visa application. The questionnaire
should be completed within 28 days of this offer email being sent to you.

All offers made by the University of Portsmouth are subject to checks for criminal convictions, tuition fee
status, regulations for students under 18 years of age, Academic Technology Approval Scheme (ATAS),
fraudulent materials, and UKVI regulations.For more information about studying at University of Portsmouth,
please visit the help pages of our website or contact us by sending an email
to globaladmissions@port.ac.uk.
