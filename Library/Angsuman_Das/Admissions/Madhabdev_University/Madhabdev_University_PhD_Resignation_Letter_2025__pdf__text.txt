Subject: Resignation from the Ph.D. Programme and discontinuation of UGC-CSIR NET JRF

Respected Sir,

With due respect, I, Angsuman Das, a Ph.D. Scholar in the Department of Zoology,
Madhabdev University, hereby submit my resignation from the Ph.D. programme of the
University due to personal and career-related circumstances.

I was admitted to the Ph.D. programme in Zoology and commenced my UGC-CSIR NET
Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) in Life Sciences on 01 June 2025. I have been availing
the fellowship through Madhabdev University since the commencement of my Ph.D.
programme.

Due to a change in my career circumstances and my intention to pursue other professional
opportunities, I am unable to continue my Ph.D. programme and associated research
activities at Madhabdev University. I therefore request the University to kindly accept my
resignation from the Ph.D. programme with effect from 01 October 2025 and discontinue my
UGC-CSIR NET JRF at Madhabdev University with effect from the same date.

I further request that my fellowship entitlement at Madhabdev University may be restricted to
the period up to 30 September 2025. I shall not claim any JRF fellowship or contingency
from Madhabdev University for any period commencing from 01 October 2025 onwards.

I also request the University to kindly complete the necessary administrative and fellowship
formalities relating to the discontinuation of my Ph.D. programme and JRF and maintain the
appropriate records regarding the period for which the fellowship and contingency were
availed by me.

I shall be grateful if my resignation is kindly accepted and the necessary
discontinuation/relieving formalities are completed.

Thanking you.
Yours faithfully,



Angsuman Das
