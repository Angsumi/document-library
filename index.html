Document Library

Records, certificates, medical files, research data and study materials, kept as downloadable originals alongside searchable PDFs and plain-text transcripts.

      OCR can contain errors, especially for handwriting. Check important details against the original. Distinct versions are kept and duplicates removed (notes, review list).

      Data: catalog.json · catalog.csv · catalog.sqlite · Source: github.com/Angsumi/document-library. Large sequencing files are served from the sequencing-data-v1 release.
